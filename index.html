Our Story

Recipes Guarded
for Generations

Auntie Gaik Lean first learned Nyonya cooking at her mother's side at the age of twelve. Every recipe she carries — every blend of spice paste, every measure of tamarind — was passed down not from a cookbook, but from memory, from touch, from love.

Today, from a beautifully restored shophouse on Bishop Street in the heart of George Town's UNESCO World Heritage zone, she still oversees every single dish that leaves her kitchen. Nothing is compromised. Nothing is shortcut.

"The Michelin star didn't change how we cook. It simply told the world what we already knew."

Signature Dishes

Each dish is a chapter from Auntie's family recipe book — prepared fresh, every service, without exception.

Pie Tee

Crispy top-hat shells filled with a fragrant stir-fry of turnip, prawn and egg, served with a sweet-tangy dip. The most-ordered first bite at Auntie's table.

Starter

Kari Kapitan

Heritage Nyonya chicken curry — free-range chicken slow-cooked in a hand-ground paste of candlenut, lemongrass, galangal and tamarind. Legendary. Irreplaceable.

Signature Main

Nasi Ulam

A fragrant medley of steamed rice, finely hand-cut herbs and spices, toasted coconut, dried shrimp, and salted fish, bursting with traditional flavours.

Heritage Rice

Gulai Tumis

Sour Nyonya fish curry — whole fish simmered in a tangy paste of eight fresh ingredients including fresh saffron, pineapple and torch ginger flower.

Our Heritage

The Soul of
Peranakan Cooking

Peranakan cuisine is the living legacy of Chinese settlers who wove their traditions into the fabric of Malay culture along the Straits. The result is one of the world's most complex, layered, and labour-intensive culinary traditions.

At Auntie Gaik Lean's, nothing comes from a packet. The gulai tumis paste is blended from eight fresh ingredients — including fresh saffron. The nasi ulam is hand-tossed with over ten herbs. Every dish is an act of preservation.

Recognised Excellence

One MICHELIN Star

Auntie Gaik Lean's became Malaysia's first Michelin-starred homestyle Peranakan restaurant — and one of only two Michelin-starred Peranakan establishments in the world. First awarded in December 2022 in the inaugural Michelin Guide Malaysia, this distinction has been retained for four consecutive years: 2022, 2023, 2024 and 2025.

Beh Gaik Lean (known as Auntie Gaik Lean) made history as the first Malaysian woman to receive a Michelin star, at the age of 69.
