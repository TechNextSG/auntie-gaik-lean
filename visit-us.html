Directions

Getting Here

By Grab or Taxi

Drop-off directly at 1 Bishop Street (Lebuh Bishop). Under 15 minutes from the Georgetown Ferry Terminal. Grab is reliable and plentiful in George Town — simply search "Auntie Gaik Lean's" in the app.

Tip: Set drop-off as "1 Bishop Street, Georgetown" to avoid confusion with the main road.

On Foot

Easily walkable from the main tourist areas of George Town.

  • → Armenian Street — 8 min walk
  • → Penang Hill Funicular — 12 min walk
  • → Chowrasta Market — 10 min walk
  • → Penang State Museum — 3 min walk

By Car

Street parking is limited on Bishop Street — we recommend using a nearby paid lot.

  • P Penang Street Car Park — 5 min walk
  • P Carnarvon Street (Jalan Carnarvon) — 5 min walk
  • P Komtar Car Park — 8 min walk

Heritage zone parking wardens are active. Always use a paid lot.

Location

You're in the Heart of It

George Town's UNESCO World Heritage Zone — one of Asia's most celebrated historic districts

UNESCO George Town Heritage Zone

You're dining inside one of Southeast Asia's best-preserved colonial heritage districts — a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 2008. Bishop Street is one of its most historic arteries.

Penang State Museum

3-minute walk from our door. The museum holds one of Malaysia's finest collections of Peranakan artefacts, antiques, and historical records. A perfect complement to your visit.

3 MIN WALK

Khoo Kongsi Clan House

5-minute walk. One of Southeast Asia's most ornate Chinese clan houses — a breathtaking monument to the Peranakan heritage that also gave birth to the food you'll eat at our table.

5 MIN WALK
