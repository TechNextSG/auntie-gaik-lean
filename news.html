Tatler Asia 2024 — Most Influential

Asia's Most Influential — Gaik Lean Beh

Tatler Asia named Beh Gaik Lean among Asia's Most Influential people of 2024 — the first Malaysian woman to earn a Michelin star, and the fourth-generation Nyonya who guards family recipes passed down from her mother. She aims to preserve her Michelin star and expand to Singapore and Jakarta.

"Fourth-generation Nyonya who started cooking at the age of 12 under tough training by her mother — guarding recipes that no cookbook has ever captured."

The Star Dec 9, 2024

Penang Peranakan Pride — Deserves Its Michelin Star

Abirami Durai profiles Chef Beh Gaik Lean (71) and son Adrian Tan. Featured: Pie Tee (RM30), Kiam Chye Ark, Egg Belanda (RM22), Nasi Ulam (RM22), Inche Kabin (RM32). Restaurant opened 2013 in a heritage shophouse formerly housing B.P. de Silva jewellers (est. 1872).

"Cooking pork-free Nyonya dishes requires a certain skill set — I have been very successful."
