Fourth Consecutive Michelin Star Retained

Auntie Gaik Lean's Old School Eatery has retained its One Michelin Star in the Michelin Guide Malaysia 2025, marking four consecutive years of recognition since 2022. An extraordinary feat for a family-run homestyle kitchen.

Heritage Dec 2022

First Malaysian Woman to Receive a Michelin Star

When Michelin Guide Malaysia launched in December 2022, Beh Gaik Lean made history at age 69 — becoming the first Malaysian woman to receive a Michelin star, and the first homestyle Nyonya cook to be recognised by the world's most prestigious culinary guide.
